# Refund policy

*Last updated: 29 September 2026*

This policy covers the one thing Flockdeck sells: the Flockdeck Remote subscription. It sits beside the
[terms of service](terms.html) and the [privacy policy](privacy.html).

## What is sold

The subscription is sold by Paddle (paddle.com), as the merchant of record: you
buy it from Paddle, which takes the payment, charges and pays the sales tax,
sends your receipts and handles refunds. Flockdeck provides the service itself.

## The free trial

Each account has a free trial of 30 days. No payment details are needed to
start it and nothing is charged during it, so there is nothing to refund.
When it ends, Flockdeck Remote stops until you subscribe.

## Refunds

- **The first payment.** If you ask within 14 days of the first payment for a
  subscription, you get a full refund of that payment. You don't need to give a
  reason.
- **After that.** Payments are not refunded for the part of a period you
  haven't used, except where the law requires it. This applies to every
  renewal payment as well: a renewal does not start a new 14 days.

## Cancelling

You can cancel at any time. Cancelling stops future charges. It takes effect at
the end of the period you have already paid for, and Flockdeck Remote carries on
until then. To cancel, use Manage subscription in Devices on a paired phone or
browser, which opens Paddle's customer portal.

## Your legal rights

None of this affects the rights the law gives you. If you are a consumer in the
UK or the EU, for example, you have a statutory right to withdraw from a
contract for a digital service, and this policy does not take it away.

## How to ask

- **Through Paddle.** Use the link in your Paddle receipt, or the Manage
  subscription or Manage transaction link in your confirmation email, or go to
  [paddle.net](https://paddle.net/). Paddle's own terms are its
  [buyer terms](https://www.paddle.com/legal/checkout-buyer-terms).
- **By writing to us.** Email **privacy@flockdeck.ai**, or write to Jim Wright,
