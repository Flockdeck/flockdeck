# Flockdeck v0.3.29

## Instant tab switching, even under load
Switching tabs used to build a fresh WebGL context for every pane in the
one you switched to, synchronously — real GPU work that got worse
exactly when your machine was busy. Switching now paints straight away
and upgrades each pane to GPU-accelerated rendering one at a time in
the background.

## Routing your very first prompt, not just fan-out and spawn
Cost-aware routing only ever looked at fanned-out tasks and spawned
helpers. A brand-new pane's first message is now routed the same way,
the moment you send it.

## Roll back to a specific version, from a list
Settings now has a proper picker for installing an earlier release,
rather than needing to know a version number for the command line.

## Verify who you're really talking to
Remote access now shows a fingerprint for each paired device and
desktop — compare it on both screens to know a relay hasn't swapped
keys on you.

## A history of your fan-outs, and getting a summary card back
Dismissing a finished fan-out's summary no longer loses it for good if
the tab does anything at all afterwards, and each project now keeps a
short history of its past fan-out jobs to look back on.

## See who's waiting on you, as a tree
The agents overview can now show every project's panes as a hierarchy,
with each helper's parent pane a click away, instead of only a flat
list.

## Close finished panes in one go
A single action clears out every idle or exited pane — and any tab
that leaves empty along with it — for the routine tidy-up most people
will reach for often.

## Loading indicators where there weren't any
Several actions that used to leave you looking at nothing while they
worked now say so.
