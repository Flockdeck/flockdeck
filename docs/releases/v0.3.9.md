# Flockdeck v0.3.9

## Pictures in the phone's chat view
Pictures in a Claude Code conversation now show up in the phone's chat view, and you can attach a picture of your own to a prompt from your phone.

## See what a waiting agent needs
When a pane is waiting on you - for a question, or a permission decision - the phone's chat view now shows what it's actually waiting for, so you can respond without switching back to your desktop.
