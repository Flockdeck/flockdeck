# Flockdeck v0.3.31

## Tab switching, the rest of the way
The earlier WebGL fix still left switching slow with nothing left to
render: xterm.js re-measuring every character cell of every terminal
in the tab you switched to, recomputed from scratch because a hidden
tab lost its cached measurement the moment it left layout. Hidden tabs
now keep their layout box — and their measurement — while still
skipping paint, so nothing is left to recompute when you switch back.

## Source available under PolyForm Noncommercial, not MIT
The desktop app's source is no longer under MIT. It's now PolyForm
Noncommercial 1.0.0: still free, still there to read and build
yourself, but for noncommercial use only. If you're rolling it out
across a company, check the licence's commercial terms first.

## "Try again" means try again
If another instance of the desktop app replaced this one's connection
to the relay, Remote access said to restart the app — even though
pressing its own "Try again" button already reconnects without one.
It now says what it does.
