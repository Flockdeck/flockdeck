# Flockdeck v0.3.45

## A new look: the Quiet deck
The desktop app has been restyled. Layout, workflows and wording are the
same; what you see and how it reads has changed.

- The default accent is now cyan, where it was blue. Blue is still there as
  one of the swatches in Settings, and a saved `teal` accent opens on the new
  default, so nothing needs choosing again.
- Status marks are shapes as well as colours: a filled circle for working, a
  hollow circle for idle or starting, a triangle for waiting or blocked, a
  filled square for failed and a hollow square for exited. You can tell the
  states apart without seeing colour. The same marks are used on tabs, in the
  top bar's count and on the rail. Working is now cyan; green is kept for
  good verdicts such as a clean worktree or a passing check.
- IBM Plex Sans is used for words and JetBrains Mono for pane details,
  counts, paths, branches and diffs. Both are built in, so nothing is
  fetched from anywhere.
- Every control that takes the keyboard shows a 2px focus ring.
- With reduced motion on in your system, the working mark stays still and
  other animations are cut to almost nothing.
- The light theme carries the whole look too.
- The app is about 126 KB larger, mostly the fonts.

**Not changed:** the terminal's own colours (its 16 ANSI colours) and its
font, and the terminal is dark in both themes. Layout sizes, ids and wording
are as before.

**Limitations:** the help pages and one tip still call a working pane
"green" or say its dot "pulses green"; that is now wrong and will be
corrected in a later release. The `⎇` and `⇢` glyphs are not in JetBrains
Mono, so they are drawn in your system's font, as they were before.

## A new logo
The mark is now Formation: five dots flying in formation, the lead dot
showing status (cyan circle working, amber triangle waiting, hollow grey
circle idle). It replaces the app icon, the Windows executable's icon and the
favicon on the site and docs.

## The exited-pane buttons work again
When a pane's process had exited, its **Restart** and **Close pane** buttons
did not respond to clicks, because the terminal's link layer sat on top of
them. They now take the click.

## A pane header keeps its figures whole
With the new type, a pane header in a two-by-two grid would have cut its
usage figures to fragments such as `221k t…`, so this release fixes that.
Empty slots in a header no longer take room, and in a header 640px wide or
more the usage, spend, limit and git figures are kept whole while the names
give way first. In narrower headers the figures still go before the names,
as before. A pane with a long name, branch and model can still cut those
names.

## Command line
- `flockdeck -quit` is no longer refused because `-agent`, or
  `FLOCKDECK_START_AGENT`, names an agent that does not exist. A stale value
  in a service's environment used to stop its own stop command working.
- The usage text for `update` now lists `-version` and `-yes`.

## Website, docs and release plumbing
- The site and docs are restyled to match, with retaken screenshots and the
  new logo.
- The Terms now give everyone the 14-day refund of a first payment, as the
  refund policy already said.
- Tooling to purge released versions from the download site was added, with a
  check of its credentials before it deletes anything. It does not change
  what you download.

Nothing needs doing to update.
